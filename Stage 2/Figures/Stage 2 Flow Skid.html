5 brass bolts × 2 washers = the 10 washers in the kit. Fit each bolt through the wall with one washer inside and one outside, then tighten the brass nut from outside until the washers just compress. Don't over-tighten plastic walls. Point the LS-01 float away from the B3 inlet stream so splashing doesn't make it chatter.

Small electrical parts

123 4567
  1. Green 2-pin screw terminal. Solder onto the D4184 PWM / GND input pads. Wire to the PCB SOLENOID_VALVE S → PWM and G → GND.
  2. Blue 3-pin screw terminal. Solder onto the three D4184 power pads at the other end. 12 V in from PCB P4, plus the SOV-01 coil leads. Check the pad names on the back of the board before wiring.
  3. 2-pin male header. Solder to the tact switch leads so it plugs into the 2-pin JST lead for ON/OFF_SWITCH. It can also serve as a plug-in alternative to terminal 1.
  4. 12 mm tact switch, red cap. The ON/OFF switch. Use two diagonally opposite legs so you always get the switched pair. Wire to SW / GND and mount it in a panel hole.
  5. D4184 MOSFET module. Optocoupler-isolated low-side switch for SOV-01. Fit a 1N4007 diode across the coil, band to +12 V, to absorb the turn-off spike.
  6. Rubber O-ring. Sealing washer for a tank bulkhead, one each side of the wall.
  7. Brass nut. Locks a brass bulkhead bolt from outside the tank.

Control sequence

  1. Check BV-03 is fully open, then press the ON/OFF switch. SOV-01 opens, P-01 runs at 50 % duty and P-02 at 100 %. TK-01 → sump → FT-01 → back to TK-01.
  2. External fluid charged into the sump lifts LS-01. P-01 stops and P-02 keeps draining the sump.
  3. When the level falls below LS-01, P-01 restarts at 50 %. FT-01 logs flow in mL/s and L/min and totalises litres returned to TK-01.
  4. For the flush, SOV-01 closes to isolate TK-01. The operator opens BV-02 by hand, which is the only manual step.
  5. P-03 starts. Flush fluid from TK-03 joins at T-01 and all three pumps run together.
  6. When TK-03 is empty, FT-01 flow drops to near zero. After a short hold time, or once the totalised flush volume reaches about 4.2 L, the controller switches every pump OFF.

Assumptions in this drawing

  1. No needle valve ships in the kit, so BV-01 on the return line acts as the throttle for the Task 2 runs at 25, 50, 75 and 100 % opening.
  2. BV-03, the third ball valve, isolates the TK-01 outlet for maintenance. It stays fully open whenever a pump runs, because it sits on P-01's suction side.
  3. TK-02's three holes are used as inlet (side A, top), LS-01 (side B, top) and outlet (side C, bottom). TK-03 uses only its bottom hole.
  4. TK-01 takes two bulkheads: its bottom outlet and the return through the lid or top.
  5. The flush-empty stop is detected through FT-01, since the kit has no second level switch.
